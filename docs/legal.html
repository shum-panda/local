About & legal

The short version: Vega is a free fan project, not an official product, and it keeps your data on your devices.

Unofficial fan project

Vega Lite is a free, unofficial fan-made tool for the ONE PIECE Card Game. It is not affiliated with, endorsed, sponsored or approved by Bandai, Toei Animation, Shueisha or Eiichiro Oda.

Trademarks and copyright

ONE PIECE © Eiichiro Oda/Shueisha, Toei Animation. The ONE PIECE CARD GAME and its card names, texts, artwork and logos are © Bandai and their respective owners. Vega uses card names, texts and images only to identify cards; it sells nothing and claims no rights to them.

Vega does not ship card images or any other data with the app: the app starts empty, and card images are loaded from optcgapi.com when a card is shown. Card images visible in the screenshots on this website belong to their owners and are shown only to illustrate the app.

Rights holder and want something changed or removed? Please open an issue.

Local card market (optional)

Only if you connect to a market server (Locals): the server gets what you share there and nothing else: your name and the contact ways you enter, your offers, the watched cards you show with your target price, messages, visits, check-ins and trades. Collection, decks and games stay on your devices. No money moves through Vega. There's no public Vega market server yet; whoever runs one is responsible for it.

Privacy

No account, no tracking, no analytics, no ads. Everything you enter (collection, decks, games, settings) is stored only on your devices, and synced between your own phone and PC if you pair them. The app talks only to the sources above, to your own PC when paired, and to a local card market server only if you connect to one.

This website is static: no cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no external fonts or scripts. Downloads are served by GitHub Releases, under GitHub's own terms and privacy statement.
