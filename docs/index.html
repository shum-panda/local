Play board & coach

Your active deck against the field: your record going 1st and 2nd, every opponent you're likely to meet and how your leader does against it. Tap an opponent for the matchup and the tech cards winners play there.

Decks that check themselves

Build or paste a list. Vega checks the deck rules, shows the cost curve and counters, compares it with tournament lists and tells you what your collection is still missing, priced with shipping.

Buy at the right moment

Sort your pack pulls

Opened a booster? Every card of the set by how much tournament lists play it and what it's worth: keep, playable, value or bulk. One tap adds a pull to your collection.

Learn from every game

Log a game in three taps or import your OPTCGSim games automatically. Vega shows your win rate going first and second, your matchups, and turn by turn whether you played on curve.
