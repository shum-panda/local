About & legal

The short version: Vega is a free fan project, not an official product, and it keeps your data on your devices.

Unofficial fan project

Vega Lite is a free, unofficial fan-made tool for the ONE PIECE Card Game. It is not affiliated with, endorsed, sponsored or approved by Bandai, Toei Animation, Shueisha or Eiichiro Oda.

ONE PIECE © Eiichiro Oda/Shueisha, Toei Animation. The ONE PIECE CARD GAME and its card names, texts, artwork and logos are © Bandai and their respective owners. Vega uses card names, texts and images only to identify cards; it sells nothing and claims no rights to them.

Vega does not ship card images or any other data with the app: the app starts empty, and card images are loaded from optcgapi.com when a card is shown. Card images visible in the screenshots on this website belong to their owners and are shown only to illustrate the app.

Rights holder and want something changed or removed? Please open an issue.

Local card market (optional)

Only if you connect to a market server (Locals): the server gets what you share there and nothing else: your name and the contact ways you enter, your offers, the watched cards you show with your target price, messages, visits, check-ins and trades. Collection, decks and games stay on your devices. No money moves through Vega. There's no public Vega market server yet; whoever runs one is responsible for it.

Privacy notice

The short version: Vega Lite has no tracking, no analytics and no ads, and what you enter stays on your devices. This notice says which data goes where when you use this website, the app, or the optional local card market.

Who is responsible

This website and the app are a private, noncommercial fan project by its maintainer (shum-panda on GitHub). Questions about your data, or a request to have something corrected or removed: please open an issue (don't put personal details in it; say how you can be reached).

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no external fonts or scripts. The pages store nothing in your browser.
  • Hosting: the site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). As with any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and the usual request data (page, time, browser) when you open a page, and keeps logs for security and operation. The maintainer has no access to these logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Downloads are files of a GitHub release and are served by GitHub in the same way.

The app

  • On your devices only: your collection, watch list and settings are stored in the app on your PC or phone. There is no Vega server that receives them, and the maintainer never sees them. Uninstalling the app (on Windows also deleting its data folder) removes them.
  • Phone and PC: if you pair them, they exchange your data directly over your own network. Nothing goes through a server on the internet.
  • Data sources: the app downloads public data onto your device: card details and images from optcgapi.com (images when a card is shown), tournament data and card lists from Limitless TCG, and the daily price files from Cardmarket. These providers receive your IP address and the request, as any website you visit does; the app sends them nothing about you or your cards. Links to Cardmarket open in your browser.
  • No telemetry: the app doesn't report usage, errors or anything else to anyone.

Locals: the local card market (optional)

  • Only if you connect to a market server. Then that server stores what you share there: the name you choose, the contact ways you enter, your offers, the cards you look for with your target prices, your messages to other players, when you say you'll come, your check-ins and your trades. Your collection as a whole stays on your devices.
  • Who sees it: the players of the locals you join see your name, your offers and the cards you look for, and the contact ways you make visible; a message is seen by the player you write to; the store that runs a local sees who is checked in and their lists. The server's operator can technically read everything stored on it.
  • Who is responsible: there is no public Vega market server. A market server is run by a store or a group of players; whoever runs it is responsible for the data on it. Ask them how long they keep it and to delete your account when you want to leave. In the app, Disconnect logs this device out; hiding your watch list or withdrawing an offer takes it off the server.
  • Automatic offers (if you turn them on) put spare copies of your collection on offer at your locals; nothing else of your collection is sent.

Your rights

Under the EU's data protection law (GDPR) you can ask whoever holds data about you what they hold, and have it corrected or deleted. For this website that is GitHub (see above); for a local card market, the operator of its server; for everything else the data is on your own devices, under your control. You can also complain to a data protection authority.
