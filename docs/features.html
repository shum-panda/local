Meta, matchups & the play board

Which leaders are played, and how they actually perform in matches, not just in final standings.

  • Meta: every leader's meta share, match win rate from real pairings, top-8 count and field score, with a matchup heatmap. Filter by time window and event size.
  • Going first or second: win rates per leader and matchup, from your logged games and your OPTCGSim games.
  • Leader pages: the average list as core, flex and tech cards, deck profiles (counters, blockers, removal, draw…), best finishes with full lists.
  • Play board & coach: your active deck against the field: your record 1st and 2nd, every likely opponent with its meta share, and per matchup the tech cards that winning lists add.

Decks & collection

Your collection is shared by all your decks: a deck only misses a card if it plays more copies than you own.

  • Deck rules checked: 50 cards, 4 copies, colors; cost curve and counters; your list compared with recent tournament lists.
  • Shopping list: what's missing, priced with shipping and checked against a budget per deck. One click watches all missing cards.
  • Paste or import: 4xOP12-034 per line, OPTCGSim decks, or copy a tournament list straight in.
  • Collection: every card you own, filtered by set, color, rarity, price or play rate, with set completion. Add a whole set as playsets.

Buy smart

Market watch

One watch list for every card you want, refreshed daily from Cardmarket's public price guide.

  • Bounty posters: copies you need, cheapest offer, trend, 30-day average, a 90-day trend line and a verdict.
  • Buy signals: Buy when the cheapest offer is at your target price or clearly under trend, or the trend is falling; Rising when it's 15 % above its 30-day average: wait if you can.
  • Shopping plan: buy cards separately or bundled? An estimate from your shipping settings (letter, tracked, fees).
  • Dashboard: what your deck still needs, cards at your chase price, deals of the day and the boosters with the best value for you.

Open packs

Pack pulls

Opening boosters? Know at a glance which pulls matter.

  • Every card of a set by how much recent tournament lists play it, how many copies you already own and what it's worth.
  • Keep: in at least 2 % of all tournament lists of the last 30 days, or in 40 % of one leader's lists (with 3+ lists). Playable: some lists play it. Value: no list plays it, but it's worth 3 € or more. Bulk: the rest.
  • +1 adds a pull to your collection; your decks and watch list update by themselves.

Get better

Game log & play analysis

Log a game in three taps, or let Vega import your OPTCGSim games from the sim's combat logs.

  • Quick log: deck, opponent, won or lost, first or second. A dice roll for the turn order is built in.
  • Performance: win rate, going first vs second, streak and form, win rate over time, your matchups next to tournament numbers.
  • Playing on curve: for every sim game, each turn's DON!!, what you spent and what your hand could have played. Missed plays are flagged.
  • Reference curves from top-8 lists and winning sim games, per leader.

Local card market

Locals

At your local game stores: who offers the cards you look for, and who looks for the ones you have.

  • Wishes from your watch list: your market watch shows there with your target prices (each card can be hidden).
  • Offers: spare copies with a price or for trade only; mark one reserved once someone takes it.
  • Chat: write in Vega (or by the contact ways you share), say when you'll come, block or report if needed.
  • Check-in: with the store's code of the day. A store can show its offers only to players who are there.
  • Trade suggestions: cards each of you looks for, as even in value as possible, with a small top-up paid in person (your limit: 10 % of the value, at least 5 €, adjustable). After the swap, Vega offers to update your collection.

You meet at the store. Nothing is paid or binding in Vega, Vega takes no fee and moves no money. The market is a server shared by the players of a local; there's no public Vega market server yet.

Two devices, one Vega

Phone ↔ PC sync

Decks, games, market watch, collection and a few settings stay the same on the Android app and the PC.

  • Each device works on its own, offline too. The phone swaps changes with the PC when it can reach it: every 5 minutes and on Sync now.
  • Pair once: turn on Phone sync on the PC, then Find my PC on the phone and enter the short code, or scan the QR code.
  • No cloud: it runs over your own Wi-Fi. Away from home, a private network like Tailscale works too.
  • Downloads once: tournaments, cards and prices come over the same connection, so the other device doesn't fetch them again.

Privacy

Local first. No account. No tracking.

Everything you enter (collection, decks, games, settings) is stored only on your devices, and synced only between your own phone and PC if you pair them.

No accountNothing to sign up for. Vega starts empty and fetches public data for you.
No trackingNo analytics, no ads, no telemetry. This website has no cookies or trackers either.
Your sourcesThe app talks only to its data sources, your own paired PC, and a local market server only if you join one.
Open data onlyVega Lite uses only data its providers make available to everyone: Limitless, optcgapi.com and Cardmarket.
