Download

Get Vega Lite

Free, for Windows and Android. Version 0.1.0. Install it on PC and phone and pair them to keep your collection and watch list in sync.

Downloads

For the ONE PIECE Card Game, built only on data its providers make available to everyone: tournaments from Limitless, cards from optcgapi.com, prices from Cardmarket, and your own cards. Locals, collection and market watch are there; meta, decks and the game log are coming.

Windows

VegaLite-windows.zip · 24 MB · Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, with Microsoft Edge (built in) or Google Chrome
Vega Lite for Windows
  1. Unzip the file anywhere you like (e.g. your Documents folder). No installer needed.
  2. Open the folder and run VegaLite.exe.
  3. Windows SmartScreen may warn because the app isn't signed: click More info → Run anyway.
  4. For phone sync, allow it on private networks when Windows asks.

Android

VegaLite-android.apk · 57 MB · Android 7.0 or newer
Vega Lite for Android
  1. Download the APK on your phone (or copy it over from your PC).
  2. Open it. Android asks to allow installing from unknown sources for your browser or file app: allow it, then go back.
  3. Tap Install. Vega isn't in an app store, so updates come from this page too.
Why not an app store? Vega is a free fan project and shared here instead. All builds are on GitHub Releases.
Requirements: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) with Microsoft Edge – built into Windows – or Google Chrome for the app window (without either, Vega opens in your default browser), about 100 MB of disk space with data. Android 7.0 or newer, about 300 MB of storage. Both need the internet for tournaments, cards and prices; Wi-Fi is best for the first download (some tens of MB).
First start: Vega starts empty and asks you to accept its terms. Then Data & settings → Update everything fetches tournaments, cards and prices onto your device.

Next: pair your phone

On the PC: Data & settings → Phone and PC, turn on Phone sync. On the phone: Find my PC, enter the code. Done.
