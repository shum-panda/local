Locals

Trade at your local game store

See who at your local offers the cards on your watch list, and who looks for your spare copies. Chat, say when you'll come, check in with the store's code. Vega suggests fair trades, with a small top-up you pay in person, and can put your spare copies on offer by itself.

You meet at the store: nothing is paid or binding in Vega, and Vega takes no fee.

Phone ↔ PC

Your PC and your phone, in sync

Your collection and watch list stay the same on both. Each device works on its own, offline too, and they swap changes over your Wi-Fi. Pair once with a short code or a QR code. No cloud in between.

Privacy

Local first. Yours only.

Vega runs on your devices. Your collection never leaves them, except to your own paired phone or PC. At your locals, others see only what you offer and look for.

No sign-up to startInstall and go. Only Locals asks for a name, on your local's market.
No trackingNo analytics, no ads, not in the app and not on this website.
OfflineEverything is stored locally; data sources are fetched on your device for you.
FreeNo price, no fees, no in-app purchases. A fan project.
