# Vega Lite

**A free, unofficial fan-made tool for the ONE PIECE Card Game.** It reads the tournament meta, keeps your decks
and collection, watches Cardmarket prices for the cards you want and logs your games – on your own PC and phone,
without an account.

- **Website:** https://shum-panda.github.io/vega-lite
- **Download:** [the latest release](https://github.com/shum-panda/vega-lite/releases/latest) – `VegaLite-windows.zip` for Windows
  10 / 11, `VegaLite-android.apk` for Android 7.0 or newer. `SHA256SUMS.txt` has the checksums.
- **Questions, bugs, a rights holder's request:** [open an issue](https://github.com/shum-panda/vega-lite/issues).

Vega Lite is not affiliated with, endorsed, sponsored or approved by Bandai, Toei Animation, Shueisha or Eiichiro
Oda. ONE PIECE and the ONE PIECE CARD GAME belong to their owners; the app shows card names, texts and pictures
only to identify cards. It uses only data its providers make available to everyone (Limitless TCG, optcgapi.com,
Cardmarket's price files) – the website's [legal page](https://shum-panda.github.io/vega-lite/legal.html) has the details.
